MAT8 D-Series is a fully automated trading software that identifies and captures momentum in US equity markets while continuously monitoring for fear and volatility signals that indicate elevated risk.

When the software detects rising fear or deteriorating market conditions, it reduces or exits exposure automatically — without any human intervention required.

The software runs on institutional-grade cloud infrastructure and deploys trades directly into your Interactive Brokers account in real time.

SIGNAL ARCHITECTURE
VOLATILITY
Fear Guard
Multi-timeframe volatility detection system. Monitors overnight fear spikes, multi-day structural elevation, and intraday deterioration — each triggering independent protective actions.
DEFENSE
Regime Signal
Defensive asset strength used as a market regime confirmation signal. Informs both entry gating decisions and active position rotation logic.
MACRO
Rate Environment Filter
Macro rate environment filter. Identifies sustained adverse rate conditions and blocks new momentum entries during hostile macro regimes.
PROTECTION LAYERS
PRE-MARKET
OVERNIGHT FEAR CHECK
Software evaluates overnight volatility conditions before market open. If fear has spiked beyond threshold, existing positions are exited prior to the open to avoid gap-down exposure.
STRUCTURAL
MULTI-DAY FEAR ELEVATION
Tracks sustained fear elevation over multiple days. Blocks new momentum entries when the market is in a structurally fearful regime, even if intraday conditions appear calm.
INTRADAY
REAL-TIME DETERIORATION EXIT
Monitors intraday fear changes continuously. If volatility rises significantly from the open, the software exits momentum positions immediately to limit intraday drawdown.
PRE-CLOSE
END-OF-DAY SAFETY CHECK
Final fear check before market close. If conditions indicate elevated overnight risk, positions are exited before the close to prevent adverse gap-down openings.
HARD STOP
INTRADAY STOP LOSS
Unconditional hard stop loss on all momentum positions. Fires regardless of any other signal state. The final line of defence against severe single-session losses.
MACRO GATE
RATE ENVIRONMENT BLOCK
Macro-level rate filter prevents new entries during sustained adverse rate cycles. Self-adjusting — normalises once the rate environment stabilises.
