ABOUT MAT8
What exactly is MAT8? +

MAT8 is a Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) business that develops and deploys proprietary algorithmic trading software. We are a technology provider — not a fund, not a financial advisor, and not an investment manager.

Our software runs on institutional-grade cloud infrastructure and executes automated trades directly in your own Interactive Brokers account based on systematic signals.

Does MAT8 provide financial advice? +

No. MAT8 does not provide financial advice, investment recommendations, or portfolio management services. We provide software only.

Deploying algorithmic trading software involves substantial financial risk. We strongly recommend consulting a licensed financial advisor before using any trading software, including MAT8.

Is MAT8 licensed or regulated? +

MAT8 operates as a SaaS software provider. We do not hold a Capital Markets Services (CMS) licence or any financial advisory licence, as we do not manage client funds or provide regulated financial services.

All trading activity occurs within your own Interactive Brokers account. You are the account holder and the decision-maker. MAT8 provides only the software tool.

ACCOUNT SETUP
What do I need to get started? +

You need an Interactive Brokers account funded and approved for trading US equity ETFs. Individual or corporate accounts both work.

A minimum capital level is recommended to ensure trading commissions remain a small proportion of position sizes. Contact us for specific guidance.

How does the software access my IBKR account? +

You grant MAT8 software access as a Third-Party Operator via IBKR's platform. This grants trade-execution access only — the software cannot withdraw funds, transfer money, or alter account settings.

You can revoke this access at any time through your IBKR Client Portal in under 60 seconds. Full custody of your funds remains with you at all times.

Can I use an existing IBKR account? +

Yes. We recommend setting up a dedicated sub-account within your existing IBKR structure so the MAT8 software operates in an isolated account, fully separate from your other holdings and strategies.

A complete onboarding guide is provided when you sign up.

RISK & RETURNS
What markets does the software trade? +

The software trades US-listed equity ETFs on major exchanges. The specific instruments used are not publicly disclosed to protect the integrity of the strategy.

You will see all trades placed in your IBKR account in real time and can review your full transaction history at any time through IBKR's platform.

What is the risk level of this software? +

The software trades leveraged instruments, which involve substantially higher risk than standard equity investing. Losses can be significant and can occur rapidly.

The software includes multiple automated protection layers designed to reduce drawdown during adverse market conditions — but no system can guarantee protection against all scenarios, including sudden market dislocations or gap-down opens.

Is the performance data on the Performance page real? +

Yes. The performance charts on this site are pulled directly from Interactive Brokers via the IBKR Flex Query API — they reflect actual live trading in real accounts, not backtests or hypothetical simulations.

The data pipeline runs automatically every day: IBKR Account → Flex Query API → Automated Sync → Live Dashboard. No manual data entry involved.

FEES & COSTS
How is MAT8 priced? +

MAT8 charges a monthly software subscription fee based on account size. There are no performance fees, no profit-sharing arrangements, and no fund management fees — it is a straightforward software licence.

Specific pricing is provided during onboarding consultation. Contact us to discuss.

What other costs will I incur? +

In addition to the MAT8 software fee, you will incur Interactive Brokers' standard trading commissions on each trade executed by the software. IBKR also charges exchange fees and regulatory fees on US equity trades.

All IBKR fees are transparent and visible in your account statements. The performance dashboard on this site reflects returns net of all IBKR transaction costs.

TECHNICAL
What happens if there is a platform outage? +

The software runs on QuantConnect's institutional cloud infrastructure with high availability. In the event of an outage, open positions remain unchanged in your IBKR account — no automatic liquidation occurs.

IBKR has its own independent risk management systems that protect accounts from extreme adverse moves regardless of third-party software status.

How does the performance dashboard update automatically? +

Every day, an automated workflow calls the IBKR Flex Query API, fetches the latest account performance data, and publishes it to the live dashboard. The full pipeline is: IBKR API → Sync Script → GitHub Actions → Live Dashboard.

The process is fully automated with no manual steps involved. Both strategy dashboards run independent pipelines.

Can I stop the software at any time? +

Yes, at any time. You can revoke the software's access to your IBKR account instantly through your IBKR Client Portal. You can also close any open positions manually through IBKR at any time — you have full control of your account independently of the software.
